## 🏏 About the Project

The **Pakistan Super League (PSL)** is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in Pakistan, founded in 2015 and organized by the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB). This project is a **PSL Management System** designed to streamline the operations of the league, including:

- **Player Auctions**: Managing bids for local and international players.
- **Team Management**: Handling team rosters, player contracts, and transfers.
- **Scheduling**: Creating and managing match fixtures and venues.
- **Venue Management**: Preparing and maintaining stadiums and facilities.
- **Ticketing**: Managing ticket sales and seating arrangements.
- **Broadcasting**: Organizing live coverage of matches.
- **Administration**: Overseeing finances, marketing, and stakeholder coordination.

This system ensures the smooth running of the league and enhances the experience for fans, players, and stakeholders.

## ✨ Features

- **Player Management**:
  - Track individual player scores, batting/bowling averages, and strike rates.
  - Update player statistics in real-time during matches.
  - Generate detailed player performance reports.

- **Team Management**:
  - Create and manage team rosters.
  - Update team data in real-time during matches.
  - Generate team performance reports.

- **Match Management**:
  - Schedule matches with date, time, location, and participating teams.
  - Update match data in real-time.
  - Generate match result reports.

- **Venue Management**:
  - Store venue details (location, seating capacity, amenities).
  - Manage venue bookings and availability.
  - Generate venue usage and revenue reports.

- **Staff Management**:
  - Store staff profiles (coaches, trainers, support personnel).
  - Manage staff contracts and assignments.
  - Track staff performance and qualifications.

- **Owner Management**:
  - Store owner details (contact information, ownership history).
  - Manage team ownership and transfers.
  - Generate financial performance reports.
